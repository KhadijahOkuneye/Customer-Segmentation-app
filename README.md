Customer Segmentation App
An interactive web app that groups mall customers into distinct segment  using **K-Means Clustering** based on their annual income and Spending Score.

**Live Demo: ** [Click here to try the app] https://customer-segmentation-app-mn6vaaml2wwmrrxgyhn3n2.streamlit.app/


## What the App Does
- Uploads the Mall Customers dataset
- Lets you choose the number of customer groups (K) with a slider
- Visualizes the clusters with an interactive scatter plot
- Helps businesses understand who their customers are


## Key INsights Found

1. High Income, High Spending : Premium Customers - target with luxury offers.
2. High Income, Low Spending : Careful spenders - need better engagement.
3. Low Income, High Spending : Impulsive buyers - great for promotions.
4. Low Income, Low Spending : Budget shoppers - focus on value deals
5. Average Income, Average Spending: The everyday majority.
